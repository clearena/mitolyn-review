What is Mitolyn?

Mitolyn is a dietary supplement that focuses on mitochondria, the parts of your cells responsible for turning food into energy. The idea behind the formula is that supporting healthy mitochondrial function may help your body use energy more efficiently.

It's not a magic pill. Like any supplement, it works best as part of a routine that includes balanced eating, movement and good sleep. Anyone promising dramatic results without lifestyle changes is overselling it.

What's inside: the 6 ingredients

Here's a plain-English breakdown of each ingredient in the formula.

Maqui Berry

A dark Chilean berry rich in anthocyanins, a type of antioxidant.

Rhodiola Rosea

An adaptogenic herb traditionally used to support energy and stress resilience.

Haematococcus

A microalgae that is a natural source of astaxanthin, a powerful antioxidant.

Amla

Indian gooseberry, used for centuries in Ayurveda and naturally high in vitamin C.

Theobroma Cacao

The cocoa plant, a source of flavonoids that support healthy circulation.

Schisandra

A berry used in traditional Chinese practice to support vitality and overall wellness.
